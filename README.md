One original dad joke (119 characters):

> I asked the elevator why it was so upbeat, and it said it spends all day lifting people's spirits, one floor at a time.

The setup is a question a father would actually ask at the dinner table. The pun does double work: "upbeat" is both cheerful and literally going up, and the punch turns on the elevator's real job — it spends all day lifting people — so "lifting people's spirits" lands as both the job description and the joke. The closer, "one floor at a time", is the surprise ending that makes the groan. One sentence, no filler, every word earning its place.
